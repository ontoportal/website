Welcome to the OntoPortal Alliance

Complete the form below to register and access member resources.

The OntoPortal Alliance is an informal, open and voluntary community gathering organisations and individuals who build, deploy or maintain semantic artefact catalogues using the OntoPortal technology. Membership is free and simply reflects the shared use of OntoPortal — whether for a running portal, a prototype, a development project or a research activity. Together we coordinate software development, share best practices and work towards interoperability between portals. There is no formal commitment: joining means being part of the conversation.
