[SocioPortal](https://socioportal.org/) is now online! Developed by UKDS for CESSDA, it is a semantic artefact catalogue dedicated to the social sciences.

Built on OntoPortal, it allows users to publish, browse and reuse ontologies, thesauri and controlled vocabularies, helping researchers and data archives discover and reuse semantic resources.
