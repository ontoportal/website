We are pleased to welcome **[TermPortal](https://portal.proyectoteresia.org/){:target="_blank"}** to the OntoPortal Alliance.

TermPortal is a terminology portal developed by the [Universidad Politécnica de Madrid (UPM)](https://www.upm.es/){:target="_blank"} in the TeresIA project. It extends the OntoPortal technology to support **OntoLex-Lemon**, so that terminologies can be published with rich lexical and linguistic information. This work was discussed at the [OntoPortal Workshop 2026]({% post_url 2026-10-19-ontoportal-workshop-2026-report %}).

With TermPortal, the Alliance now brings together 11 public portals. See them all on the [About page]({{ site.baseurl }}/about/).
