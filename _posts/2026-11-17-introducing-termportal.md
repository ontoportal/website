[TermPortal](https://portal.proyectoteresia.org/metabuscador/) is now available as a portal for publishing, exploring and reusing terminological resources. Developed by the Ontology Engineering Group ([OEG](https://oeg.fi.upm.es/)) at the Universidad Politécnica de Madrid ([UPM](https://www.upm.es/)), in collaboration with [librAIry](https://librairy.es/), as part of the [TeresIA](https://proyectoteresia.org/) project, it provides unified access to terminology resources in Spanish and Spain's co-official languages.

Built on OntoPortal, TermPortal extends the platform with support for **OntoLex-Lemon**, alongside OWL and SKOS. It allows users to explore terms, definitions, lexical variants and translations, while terminology providers can publish and manage their resources. The portal also provides access through a REST API.

As an example of how these terminological resources can be used in AI-powered applications, TeresIA also provides a [conversational interface](https://wiig.dia.fi.upm.es/teresia-chat/) that allows users to explore vocabularies and query terms, definitions, synonyms and translations using natural language.

We invite terminology specialists, researchers and language technology developers to explore TermPortal and share their feedback.

TeresIA was funded by the Spanish Ministry for Digital Transformation and Public Administration through Spain's Recovery, Transformation and Resilience Plan, with funding from the European Union (NextGenerationEU).
